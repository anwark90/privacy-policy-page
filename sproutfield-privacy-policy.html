Sproutfield Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Sproutfield is a farming game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version

Sproutfield does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your saved farm

Your farm's progress (coins, crops, buildings and settings) is saved only on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes the saved farm.

Internet connection

The game is fully contained in the app and plays without an internet connection. It does not send any data over the internet.

Children

Sproutfield is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Apple

Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated statistics about downloads and crashes, but only if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device's settings. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
