Point of Sale – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This policy explains how the Point of Sale app (“the app”) handles information. The app is operated by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS NAME] (“we”, “us”).

What the app does

Point of Sale is a till, stock and sales app for shops. It connects to your shop’s own EPOS system so that sales, stock levels, orders and end-of-day figures stay in sync.

Information the app handles

  • Shop data you enter or scan: products, barcodes, prices, stock levels, sales, payments recorded at the till, orders and end-of-day totals. This data is sent between the app and the EPOS system it is connected to, and is used only to run your shop.
  • Camera: the app asks for camera access only to scan barcodes. Images from the camera are processed on your device to read the barcode. They are not saved and are not sent anywhere.
  • Settings: connection and display settings are stored on your device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use third-party analytics in the app.
  • The app does not store payment card numbers.

Where your data is stored

Shop data is stored in the EPOS system your app connects to, which is controlled by the shop owner. Data on your device is stored in the app’s own storage and is removed when you delete the app.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the app, including using secure connections where the EPOS system supports them. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Shop data is kept in your EPOS system for as long as the shop owner chooses. To have data removed, contact the shop owner or contact us using the details below and we will help where we can.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under UK data protection law, you may have the right to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. Contact us to make a request.
